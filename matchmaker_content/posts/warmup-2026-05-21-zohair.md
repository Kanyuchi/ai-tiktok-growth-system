I've been to a lot of conferences.

I can count the meetings that actually changed something on one hand.

The rest? A tote bag of business cards, 40 connection requests I never followed up on, and the quiet feeling that the one person I needed to meet was probably in the room — three conversations away — and I never found them.

Here's what nobody admits about networking: the problem was never access. It was discovery. 2,500 brilliant people and no way to know which five matter to *you* before you've already flown home.

We spent the last few months building something to fix exactly that for Proof of Talk 2026.

Tuesday, I'll show you what it does.

Louvre Palace · June 2–3.

# Community-Reply Playbook

How we respond to comments on the team's posts and to mentions of "Proof of Talk Matchmaker."
**Posture:** I draft every reply into a dated queue → you approve → it posts. Nothing auto-posts (both platforms human-gated) until the upgrade gate clears (see campaign plan §6).

## Voice rules
- **One or two sentences.** A premium B2B audience reads fast; long replies look needy.
- **Warm, never defensive.** Skeptics get the reframe, not a rebuttal.
- **Never salesy-desperate.** No "Don't miss out!!!" No emoji storms (one tasteful emoji max).
- **Always a next step when natural** — a link, a DM ask, or a question back — but not forced.
- **Match the commenter's register.** A VC gets crisp; an excited attendee gets warm.
- **Names matter.** Open with their first name when you have it.
- **Never invent facts** (match counts, attendee names, speaker confirmations). If unsure, route to a human.

## Comment taxonomy → response patterns

| Type | Pattern | Example reply |
|---|---|---|
| **"How does it work?"** | 1-line explainer + link | "It reads every attendee's profile and surfaces the five who matter most to you — with the reason each meeting's worth it. Have a look: meet.proofoftalk.io" |
| **"I'm coming!" / excited** | warm + activation nudge | "Love it, {name} — see you at the Louvre. Your matches are already waiting in your inbox; open the magic link and they're right there." |
| **Skeptic ("just another conf app")** | the reframe, no defense | "Fair — most are. The difference here is it doesn't hand you a list, it tells you *which* five and *why*. Worth 30 seconds: [video/link]." |
| **VC / founder self-promo** | bridge to the value | "{name}, that's exactly the kind of profile the matchmaker reads for — it'd line you up with the people actively looking for what you do." |
| **Question about tickets/price/logistics** | route + link | "Everything's here, {name} → proofoftalk.io. Happy to connect you with the team if you want specifics." |
| **Press / journalist / partner inquiry** | DO NOT auto-engage | Flag in queue as `ESCALATE → Shaun/Zohair`. Draft only an internal note, not a public reply. |
| **Negative / complaint** | acknowledge + take it private | "Sorry that's been your experience, {name} — DM me and I'll make it right." Flag `ESCALATE`. |
| **Spam / bots** | `SKIP` | no reply |

## Routing rules
- **Escalate, never auto-reply:** press, partnerships, complaints, legal/privacy questions, anything naming a specific attendee or speaker.
- **Founder-only:** if Zohair is tagged personally in a substantive thread, draft for *him* and mark `→ Zohair`.

## Cadence
- During launch week: check 2–3×/day (mid-morning, mid-afternoon, evening Paris).
- Reply within ~2h during business hours — fast warm replies are the whole point of "before you even land."
